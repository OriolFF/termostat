Privacy policy

Termostat · last updated 9 November 2026

What the app accesses

With your permission, Termostat uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read your Nest thermostat's state (temperature, humidity, mode, setpoints and heating status) and to send the changes you make (target temperature, mode, Eco).

Where your data goes

Nowhere except your own Mac. Termostat runs entirely on your computer and talks only to Google's APIs. It has no server, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties.

  • Your Google sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac.
  • Temperature readings are saved to a local history file on your Mac to draw charts.

Removing access

Sign out in Termostat's settings, or revoke access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions or from the Nest partner connections page. Deleting the app's folder in ~/Library/Application Support/Termostat removes the local history.

Google API data

Termostat's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
